# Refund Policy

Customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of the
initial purchase.

Annual subscriptions can receive a full refund within 30 days of the initial
purchase.

Renewals are non-refundable.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take up to 10 business
days to appear on the statement.

To request a refund, open a ticket from the billing section of the dashboard.
