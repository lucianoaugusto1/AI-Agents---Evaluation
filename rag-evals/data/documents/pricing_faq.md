# Pricing FAQ

How do I change my subscription plan? Open the billing section of the
dashboard and pick a new plan. The change applies on the next cycle.

Do you offer annual billing? Yes, an annual subscription is available for
every plan and is charged once per year.

Is the annual subscription discounted? Yes, the annual subscription has a
discount, and the exact discount percentage is listed on the plans page.

Can I pay with a purchase order? Purchase orders are available for Enterprise
customers only.
